## Topics

- Basic notions
- Trees
- Connectivity
- Eulerian and Hamiltonian cycles
- Matchings
- Planar graphs
- Graph colourings
- The matrix tree theorem
- Kuratowski’s theorem
- Ramsey theory
- Extremal problems

## Prerequisites
